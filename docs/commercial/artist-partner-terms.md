# Artist Partner Terms (Draft)

**Status:** Draft — not a signed agreement  
**Owner:** Ikram & Co. / Dobeu Tech Solutions LLC  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-15

This document captures recommended default split bands for artist partnerships. Final terms are owner-confirmable per artist and fulfillment type. Nothing here is a binding MSA or executed contract.

## Scope

Applies to catalog works sold through the Ikram & Co. storefront (originals, limited editions, and print-on-demand / POD stickers). Fulfillment partners (e.g. Printful) and payment processors (Stripe) sit outside the artist share calculation until contribution margin is known.

## Recommended artist net splits

Percentages are **artist net after payment fees** (Stripe), unless noted.

| Fulfillment type | Artist share (recommended default) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Originals** (1 of 1) | **50–70%** | Higher band for scarce works; confirm shipping/insurance risk allocation separately. |
| **Limited editions** | **30–50%** | Band reflects print run size, edition management, and inventory risk. |
| **POD / stickers** | **Design fee** *or* **% of contribution margin** after Printful + ship + return allowance + Stripe | Do **not** default to a raw % of list price. |

### POD grill note

Refuse an **80% POD artist share** if Printful + Stripe already consume roughly **40–55%** of list without raising list price or cutting SKU cost. That structure leaves the house underwater before marketing or returns.

Prefer either:

1. A fixed **design / licensing fee** per SKU or drop, or  
2. A share of **contribution margin** after Printful + shipping + expected returns + Stripe — not of GMV.
